Install a diorama

Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  1. Plug the device into your computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. Press Install under the diorama you want.
  3. Pick the device in the list and wait for “Installation complete” (about 2–4 minutes). Don't unplug the cable while it installs.
